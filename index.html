Guangcheng Zhu 祝广程

College of Computer Science and Technology
Zhejiang University

I am a master’s student at Zhejiang University, jointly advised by Haobo Wang and Tianlei Hu. My research focuses on data-efficient post-training for large language models. I aim to build stronger models with less training data and fewer human annotations. My current interests include data selection, semi-supervised reinforcement learning, and fine-grained supervision.
